
# Golf Ball Simulator in Scilab (Scilab, Python and C++)

Language: **EN** · [FR](README.md)

> **Abstract — guiding principle.** *Directly measure what radars can actually measure, then use the physical model to identify the quantities that are not directly observable.* A 10.525 GHz Doppler radar measures frequencies (radial velocities, patch micro-Doppler, R1/R2 stereo curvature) — not club angles or a 3D spin vector. So we first estimate, as accurately as possible, the radar observables: ball speed (vBall), club head speed (V club), smash factor (smash = vBall/V club), total spin (rpm) by multi-channel fusion, initial azimuth (α₀) and azimuthal curvature (Δα), and launch angle (take-off angle ψ) by least-squares (fit) ballistic fitting; then the **physical model** (club→ball impact + flight with drag/Magnus/gravity) acts as an inverse: a sweep of the face/path angle (`GamaFacePath`) constrained by the measured curvature decomposes backspin/side spin, dynamic loft deduced from ψ, etc. Each non-observable quantity is thus identified by measurement ↔ simulation consistency, never invented. Results on 12 real captures: vBall 6.3 %, spin 20.5 %, carry 10.4 % mean error ([details in `Cpp/README_EN.md`](Cpp/README_EN.md)).

## CPP simulator
Application `simu_headless --capture` (interactive 3D scene + radar measurements panel + docked/zoomable spectrogram):
```shell
cd Cpp/build
./simu_headless --capture ../../sci/2019-02-24-F7-123m-150kmh-5274trm-210trm-cs111.csv F7 1.0 --caseangle_deg 11 --open
```
The **launch angle ψ is estimated directly from the capture** (ballistic fit of the post-impact ridge: smoothed instantaneous frequency of the ball lobe, 2D flight with White drag + gravity + Magnus, ψ = argmin SSE up to a free scale — ±0.75° on synthetic captures of known truth). Options: `--launch_deg 16` to force ψ, `--caseangle_deg 11` if the suitcase tilts the radar upward (v_r = V·cos(ψ−ε), ε added to the fit — the two options are mutually exclusive), `--live 2.0` to record the sound card (radar on audio input, saves `live_capture.wav` then analyzes it). Measured ψ drives the simulation when conf ≥ 0.5 (or `--caseangle_deg` given), otherwise fallback to the club loft.

<img src="sci/img/capture_simu_headless_20261110.png" width="980">

**Total spin: fusion of 7 channels** (details in `Cpp/README_EN.md` Genesis §3/§7): ① patch (Hilbert envelope), ② phase (instantaneous frequency), ③ comb (spectrum autocorrelation), ④ excursion β (confidence ×0.25), ⑤ micro-Doppler sideband frame by frame (parabolic interpolation, IC gating), ⑥ smoothed spectral density "Scilab-style" (frame-rebased, chirp-aware, conf ×0.25), ⑦ loft(ψ) (model spin `V0·cosψ·sin(loft_dyn)·CoeffBackSpin`, active only if loft_dyn ∈ 12°–35°). The reference is the most confident channel, confidence-weighted average of channels within ±15 %. **Results on 12 captures** (`Python/validate_all.py`): vBall 6.3 %, **spin 20.5 %** (18.2 % with `--caseangle_deg 11`), carry 10.4 %. The 3D animation shows at impact a **square**: orange tube = face plane (perpendicular to launch), red tube + arrow = face normal, pointing toward the curve side.

## Radar setup — geometry, swing line and shot angles

<img src="sci/img/setup_radar.png" width="980">

Top view: the two 10.525 GHz Doppler radars (baseline b = 0.30 m) aim at the patched ball (L₀ ≈ 1.50 m).
`f_C = (f₁+f₂)/2` → radial velocity (`V_r = f_D / 19.49` km/h) ; `f_Δ = (f₁−f₂)/2` → azimuth `α(t) = arctan[(2L/b)·(f_Δ/f_C)]`.
At the shot: the spin axis ω is tilted by β → backspin = ω·cos β, sidespin = ω·sin β (draw/slice, curvature Δα).
